Your Information.

Diagnostics .

We use advanced diagnostic tools to read fault codes, monitor live data, and pinpoint issues accurately. From engine warnings to electrical faults, we don't guess, we test, trace, and explain the problem clearly, so you know exactly what needs done and why before any work begins.

  • Fault code scanning and live data analysis
  • Electrical and engine issue tracing
  • Clear explanation before any work begins

Tyres .

We supply and fit tyres suited to your vehicle and driving needs, checking tread depth, pressure, and wear patterns. We also inspect for alignment issues and damage that could affect safety, ensuring your tyres provide proper grip, handling, and longevity on the road.

  • Tyre fitting, pressure, and tread checks
  • Inspection for wear patterns and damage
  • Advice on suitable tyres for your vehicle

Servicing .

Our servicing covers oil and filter changes, fluid top-ups, and full vehicle checks in line with manufacturer standards. We inspect brakes, suspension, and key components to keep everything running smoothly, helping prevent costly issues and keeping your vehicle reliable.

  • Oil, filters, and fluid checks/replacement
  • Brake, suspension, and safety inspections.
  • Maintenance in line with manufacturer standards

Batteries .

We test battery health, charging systems, and connections to diagnose starting or electrical issues. Whether it's a simple replacement or a deeper fault, we ensure the correct battery is fitted and performing properly, so your vehicle starts reliably every time.

  • Battery health and charging system testing
  • Replacement with correct specification battery
  • Checks on terminals and electrical connections

Exhausts .

We inspect exhaust systems for leaks, corrosion, and damage that can affect performance and emissions. From minor repairs to full replacements, we ensure everything is secure, efficient, and compliant, helping your engine run cleanly and quietly.

  • Inspection for leaks, rust, and damage
  • Repairs or full system replacements
  • Emissions and performance checks

Repairs .

We carry out a wide range of repairs, from worn brakes to faulty components, using quality parts and proven methods. Every job is approached with care and attention, ensuring the issue is properly fixed, not just temporarily solved, so your vehicle stays dependable.

  • Brake, suspension, and component repairs
  • Fault fixing using quality parts
  • Long-term solutions, not temporary fixes
